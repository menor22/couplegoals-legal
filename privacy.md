## 3. What information do we collect?

We collect only the information necessary to operate the App. Specifically:

### 3.1 Information you provide directly

- **Account information:** your email address (used for sign-in) and a display name of your choosing. We do not require your real name.
- **Authentication identifiers:** if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a unique identifier and your email address from those services. We do not receive your password.
- **Couple data:** when you pair with a partner, we store the link between your account and theirs.
- **Content you create:** the challenges, to-dos, events, reactions ("Sparks"), and notes you create within the App. This content is shared between you and your paired partner.
- **Optional inputs:** event titles, dates, optional notes, optional person names (e.g. whose birthday).
- **Purchase information (if you subscribe):** payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your card or bank details, your Apple ID, or your billing address. Through RevenueCat (see Section 6.5) we receive the state of your subscription: which plan you bought, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, whether it will renew, whether a payment problem or a refund occurred, the store, and Apple's transaction identifiers. It is linked to your account identifier, not to your name or email. Your partner sees that your couple is unlocked, and by whom, but never any payment details.
- **Profile photo (optional):** if you add a profile photo, it is stored in Firebase Storage, downscaled to a maximum of 512 pixels, visible only to you and your paired partner, and deleted when you delete your account.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device information:** device type, operating system version, app version, and device language. Used to ensure the App functions correctly on your device.
- **Push notification tokens:** a unique token generated by Apple Push Notification service (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver notifications to your device. You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.
- **Usage analytics (pseudonymous):** with PostHog (see Section 6.2) we record how the App is used: actions such as "logged a challenge", "opened Settings", "opened a notification" or "saw the subscription screen", the screens you visit, subscription and trial events (started, renewed, cancelled, expired, refunded, payment problem; trial started and ended), and technical error reports when something goes wrong in the App. Each event is linked to a random account identifier and, once you are paired, a random couple identifier — never to your name, email or photo, and never with anything you write (challenge titles, notes, messages). Events also carry technical context: device model, operating system and app version, language, time zone, network type (Wi-Fi or mobile) and whether the App is a test version. Prices are never recorded. PostHog is set not to store your IP address, and we do not use it to locate you. You can turn this off at any time in the App (Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics): from then on, no usage events are recorded about you, from the App or from our servers.
- **Invite links and invite codes:** when you open an invite link (couplegoals.ch/invite/), the page counts one visit: the type of device (iPhone, Android or other), the page language, and whether the link contained a code. The code itself never leaves your browser. This count is sent to PostHog without any identifier that could link it to you or to other visits, and without your IP address. To limit abuse, our server counts attempts: visits to the invite page per IP address and, when you enter your partner's code in the App, attempts per account, per IP address and per code entered. IP addresses and codes are stored only as a keyed hash (a one-way transformation using a secret key), never in readable form, together with the times of the attempts. This data is deleted automatically, normally within two days of the last attempt. The invite page sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

### 3.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect: your contacts, location, microphone, camera, biometric data, health data, payment card or bank details, or browsing history outside the App. (The only image we store is an optional profile photo you choose to add — see Section 3.1.)

## 4. How do we use your information?

We use your information to:

- Provide the core functionality of the App (create accounts, pair partners, store and sync your shared content, deliver notifications).
- Give you and your partner access to the paid features you subscribed to, keep that access in sync when you change partners or restore a purchase on another device, and detect when both of you pay for the same couple so we can tell you.
- Send notifications about your partner's activity (with your consent — managed in your device's notification settings).
- Suggest reminder times for your challenges and write the texts of your notifications and weekly recap (see Section 6.4).
- Maintain security, prevent fraud, and resolve technical issues.
- Understand how the App is used in aggregate, so we can improve it.
- Communicate with you about account issues or material changes to the App (only when necessary).

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

## 6. Who do we share your data with?

We share data with the following third-party service providers, who act as processors on our behalf:

### 6.1 Firebase (Google LLC)
We use Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Firestore (data storage), Cloud Functions (server logic), and Cloud Messaging (push notifications). Your data is stored in Firebase's European multi-region (`eur3`), which spans data centers in Belgium and the Netherlands. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 6.2 PostHog (PostHog Inc.)
We use PostHog Cloud (EU instance) for product analytics. Analytics data is stored in Frankfurt, Germany. PostHog's privacy policy: https://posthog.com/privacy

### 6.3 Apple Push Notification service (Apple Inc.)
Used to deliver push notifications to your device. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

### 6.4 Anthropic (Anthropic PBC)
To suggest reminder times for your challenges and to write the texts of the notifications and the weekly recap about them, we send Anthropic's Claude API the titles of your non-private challenges, together with anonymous counts (how many days a challenge was done, on which weekdays and at what time of day). We never send names, account identifiers, notes, messages between you and your partner (including Sparks), reward or forfeit texts, or private challenges. Challenges you choose from the built-in catalog, with their title unchanged, are not sent. Under its Commercial Terms, Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and may not use it to train its models. Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

### 6.5 RevenueCat (subscriptions)
We use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) to manage subscriptions bought through the App Store. RevenueCat receives your account's technical identifier (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription information from Apple, and technical data (device model, app version, operating system, country, IP address). This data is hosted in the United States. The transfer is governed by RevenueCat's data processing agreement, which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, adapted for Swiss law (FADP). Subscription events (for example "subscription started" or "renewed", without prices or payment details) are also recorded in our analytics (Section 6.2) under the same account identifier. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple processes your payment as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties. We do not share data for advertising purposes.

## 7. Where is your data stored?

Your data is stored primarily within the European Union (Belgium, the Netherlands, and Germany), via the providers listed in Section 6. Some metadata may transit through other regions for technical reasons (e.g. Apple Push Notification routing). Our providers have certified safeguards for any such transfers (Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions where applicable).

One feature is an exception to this EU-first storage. The data described in Section 6.4 (titles of non-private challenges and anonymous counts) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API and processed on Anthropic's infrastructure in the United States. No name, account identifier, or other content is attached. Our use of the Claude API is governed by Anthropic's Commercial Terms of Service, which incorporate Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum by reference; that Addendum incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK and Swiss equivalents) to the extent required by applicable data protection law, as the safeguard for this transfer. Challenges you choose from the built-in catalog are not sent.

Subscriptions are a second exception. RevenueCat receives the data described in Section 6.5 in the United States; the App connects to RevenueCat when it starts, even if you never subscribe. No content you create in the App (challenges, to-dos, events, notes, photos) is sent. The transfer is governed by RevenueCat's data processing agreement, which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, adapted for Swiss law (FADP).

## 8. How long do we keep your data?

- **Active accounts:** for as long as your account exists.
- **Deleted accounts:** when you delete your account in the App, your personal data is removed from our active databases within 30 days. Some data may persist briefly in encrypted backups (up to 90 days) before being permanently overwritten.
- **Purchase information:** for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, our copy is deleted and we ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record. Apple keeps its own records of your purchases under its privacy policy, and we may keep financial reports from Apple that do not identify you, as accounting law requires.
- **Analytics data:** usage events are kept for 12 months and then deleted. When you delete your account, your analytics profile is deleted; events already recorded stay until that period ends, linked only to the random account identifier, and no new events are recorded.
- **Abuse-prevention counts:** the attempt counts described in Section 3.2 (invite links and invite codes) are deleted automatically, normally within two days of the last attempt.
- **Data sent to Anthropic (Section 6.4):** Anthropic deletes it within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag it as violating Anthropic's usage policy (then it may be kept for up to 2 years) or the law requires longer retention. The reminder times and texts it produces are stored with your challenge and deleted with it.

## 10. Account deletion

You can delete your CoupleGoals account at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete Account). We display a clear confirmation before deletion. **This action cannot be undone.**

**What is deleted.** Your profile and profile photo, your check-ins, the challenges you created on your own, and the to-dos, lists and events you added. A list you created is kept only if your partner added items to it: it is then transferred to your partner, and the items you added are deleted. Your analytics profile is deleted too, and nothing more is recorded about you; usage events already recorded are kept only until the end of their retention period (Section 8), linked to a random identifier, not to your name or email.

**What your partner keeps.** CoupleGoals is a paired application, so some data is shared. Deleting your account does not delete your partner's own data: their check-ins, their own challenges, and the to-dos, lists and events they added stay with them. Challenges you were doing together continue as theirs. Past challenges stay in their history, where you appear only as "Deleted account", without your name or photo. Bets between you end without a result: no one wins or loses, and no reward or forfeit applies.

**Notice to your partner.** Your partner is told once, in the App, that your account was deleted, using your display name. The name is removed as soon as they have seen the notice, and at the latest after 30 days.

**Subscriptions.** Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: Apple keeps billing until you cancel it in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). The App reminds you of this before you delete your account, with a link to those settings. Your purchase information is deleted with your account (Section 8).

After deletion, your personal data is removed from our active databases within 30 days. See Section 8 for details on backup retention.

## 11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS for all network requests).
- Encryption at rest (provided by Firebase, PostHog and RevenueCat infrastructure).
- Access controls limiting who can read your data (only you and your paired partner can see your shared content; only authorized administrators can access infrastructure).
- Regular security reviews and updates.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority in accordance with applicable law (within 72 hours under GDPR).
